# Launch video: narration and scene list

About a minute: what Vibe Manager is and who it is for (from `docs/vision.md`), cut from the demo's real footage plus title cards. It says only what release v0.1.0 does (`docs/releases/v0.1.0.md`). Same format as `demo-script.md`; a scene's `Shows:` names the demo shots it reuses.

## l01 card

Shows: title card "Vibe Manager" and the line "Where your company's AI-built internal tools run".

> People across your company, and their coding agents, are already writing small internal tools. Where should those tools run, and who may open them?

## l02 term

Shows: footage of d03 (`docker compose up -d`, `docker compose ps`) and d06 (`vibe admin connect-oidc`).

> Vibe Manager is an open-source, self-hosted platform for them. IT installs it once, on its own servers, and connects the company's sign-in.

## l03 term+web

Shows: footage of d09 (`vibe new hello`) and d10 (`vibe dev`, the test User picker).

> An employee, or their coding agent, makes an application with one tool: create it, and run it on their own computer as a test user.

## l04 term+web

Shows: footage of d11 (`vibe deploy`) and d12 (the Application at its own address, the Dashboard).

> One command deploys it to its own address, behind the company's sign-in.

## l05 term+web

Shows: footage of d13 (Grace refused), d14 (`vibe share`) and d15 (Grace opens it).

> It opens for its builders only, until they share it with a person, a group, or everyone.

## l06 card

Shows: card "Shared databases, governed by IT": Grants, the Operation log, the Change history.

> Applications reach the company's shared databases only as their grants allow, as the person using them, and every operation is recorded.

## l07 card

Shows: end card "Vibe Manager 0.1. Open source, self-hosted. A first release: for your own applications and data you can lose." and the repository's address.

> Version zero point one is a first release, to try with applications you wrote yourself. Get started from the repository.
