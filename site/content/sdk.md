# The SDK's types

An Application is written with the SDK, `@vm/sdk`. These are its types, `sdk/index.d.ts`, as they are in the repository, cut into their parts. A project made by `vibe new` has them already; its `AGENTS.md` tells you and your coding agent how to work in it.

The words are those of the [Glossary](glossary/): a Manifest, a Resource and its Access level, the Trusted context, the Change history.
