# Sharing

This is step 9 of [Getting started](getting-started/). Contributors may change and deploy an Application. It opens for them alone until one of them shares it with people, Groups or everyone. The Dashboard, at `https://dashboard.vibe.example.com/`, lists the Applications you contribute to and those shared with you, says what of each will not work for you, and lets a Contributor share one. `vibe unshare` and `vibe contributors` are on [The tool's commands](builders/commands/#sharing-and-contributors).
