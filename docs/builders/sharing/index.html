For Builders

Sharing

This is step 9 of Getting started. Contributors may change and deploy an Application. It opens for them alone until one of them shares it with people, Groups or everyone. The Dashboard, at https://dashboard.vibe.example.com/, lists the Applications you contribute to and those shared with you, says what of each will not work for you, and lets a Contributor share one. vibe unshare and vibe contributors are on The tool's commands.

#9. Share it

Let others open it. Share it with a person by their email address (they must have signed in to the installation once), with a Group, or with everyone who can sign in:

sh
vibe share hello --email colleague@example.com
vibe share hello --group engineering
vibe share hello --everyone
